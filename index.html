Obsidian desktop plugin · Public beta 0.8.2

Visual Math

I developed a plugin for writing mathematical and scientific notation directly in Obsidian notes. It combines Visual, LaTeX, and Hybrid equation editing with local graphing and numerical calculus.

It also includes chemistry notation, physics and unit tools, and English, Chinese, and Spanish interfaces. Desktop only; numerical tools have supported-domain limits.

Published Chrome extension

Evidence Lens

I built Evidence Lens to make the evidence behind a webpage easier to inspect. It identifies the article’s genre and brings source, transparency, and text signals into one place.

Analysis runs locally in English and Chinese, with downloadable HTML summaries for reviewing the findings.

Conference presentation

October 23, 2026

2026 GCIC Fall Undergraduate Research Conference

Money is the Cattle’s Feed: A Regression Analysis of Macroeconomic Factors Influencing U.S. Cattle Export Markets

I presented my economics research at HCC Northeast Campus, Codwell Hall. The presentation examines associations between macroeconomic conditions and U.S. beef export prices, comparing current-month variables with a four-month corn-price lag.

ECONH 2301 · Professor Shuang Xu

The models examine associations with prices, rather than causal effects or export volume. This presentation is a later revision of the written honors study linked below.

ECONH 2301

U.S. Beef Export Markets

Professor Shuang Xu

The Impact of Macroeconomic Factors and Production Lags on the U.S. Beef Export Market (2010–2020).

Multiple linear regression using monthly economic data, with attention to model assumptions and interpretation.
